The 4 Evaluation Topics (60 Points)

Every submission is transcribed and objectively evaluated against 4 mandatory pillars.

1

Who are you at your strongest?

Weight: 15 Pts

Core strengths, capability demonstrations, and actual SD context rather than CV regurgitation.

2

What are you proudest of so far?

Weight: 20 Pts

2–3 meaningful achievements, measurable scale, personal ownership, and before/after metrics.

3

Where are you heading (5 yrs)?

Weight: 10 Pts

Aspiration clarity, future capability targets, and alignment with Ericsson's evolving delivery model.

4

Idea to Accelerate Ericsson

Weight: 15 Pts

Problem definition, execution practicality, scalability, and projected quantifiable business value.
